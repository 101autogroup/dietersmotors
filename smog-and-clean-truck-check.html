Who Needs a Clean Truck Check?

California requires periodic emissions compliance testing for most non-gasoline vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR operating in the state. That includes:

  • Landscapers, plumbers, electricians and general contractors
  • Delivery, courier and box truck fleets
  • Owner-operators and single-truck businesses
  • Agricultural and municipal vehicles

Failed Your Clean Truck Check?

Test and repair happen under one roof. We diagnose and repair the faults that most often cause a diesel truck to fail:

  • Diesel particulate filter (DPF) faults
  • NOx sensor and SCR/DEF system faults
  • Exhaust leaks and high opacity readings
  • Check engine lights, derates and limp mode
