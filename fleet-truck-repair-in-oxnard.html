Fleet repair questions, answered.

How do the discounted fleet rates work?

Fleet work is billed at a reduced labor rate compared to retail. Call to set up an account and we'll confirm your rate up front, so every estimate you get afterward already reflects it.

How many trucks do I need to qualify?

Fewer than you'd think. If you run trucks commercially, ask us — small two- and three-truck outfits get fleet treatment here.

Do you do Clean Truck Check inspections?

Yes. We handle California HD I/M Clean Truck Check inspections for medium- and heavy-duty diesel trucks, and we can diagnose and repair the faults that cause a failure.

How big a truck can you take?

We service light- and medium-duty work trucks, vans and box trucks. Call with your make, model and GVWR and we'll tell you straight whether it fits our bays.

Can you keep our maintenance records?

Yes. Fleet accounts get per-vehicle service history, so you know what was done, when, and what's coming due next.
