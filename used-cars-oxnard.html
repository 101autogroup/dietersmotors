02 — Buyer checklist

What to check before you buy any used car

  • Have it inspected by a mechanic before you sign — ours is next door
  • Scan for stored codes, not just a dark check engine light
  • Check brakes, tires and suspension wear
  • Look for fluid leaks and cooling-system condition
  • Ask what maintenance is due soon
  • Know where you'll get it serviced after the sale
